Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal assistant that runs only on its owner's own Windows PC. It is used by its owner alone and is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data Jarvis uses

When the owner signs in with Google, Jarvis can read the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, add labels to mails, save reply drafts, and, only after the owner says yes for that one mail, send a reply, archive a mail or move it to Trash.

Where the data stays

  • Mail and calendar data is processed on the owner's PC. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The Google sign-in is kept in Windows Credential Manager on that PC and is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • When the owner asks for a reply draft, the text of that one mail may be sent to an AI model the owner has chosen to write the draft. Nothing else is shared.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Jarvis at any time in Jarvis (Desk, Connections) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the saved sign-in from the PC.
